Vietnam's Law on Support for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises defines the general framework for supporting SMEs. The law covers principles, support contents, resources, and responsibilities of authorities and organizations involved in SME support.

For a small enterprise, this type of policy may be relevant when the business wants to access support programs, consulting, training, innovation support, credit-related support, or other government-backed assistance. A bank can use this information to guide SME customers toward suitable support channels.

Typical documents that may be needed include business registration information, proof of SME status, financial statements, and a short description of business needs. The impact is higher when a business is applying for financing or wants to join an SME support program.
