title: Loan Renewal Document Check
topic: loan
source_id: SYNTHETIC
---

When an SME renews a business loan, the bank may request updated documents to evaluate business health and repayment capacity. These documents may include recent cash flow reports, bank statements, revenue proof, invoice records, tax payment proof, and an updated business plan.

The impact is high if the SME has an active loan, the renewal deadline is near, and important documents are missing. The impact is medium if the SME has no immediate deadline but still has incomplete documents. The impact is low if the SME has no loan or the policy is unrelated to financing.

Recommended actions include preparing cash flow reports for the latest months, organizing invoice records, checking tax payment status, and contacting the bank relationship manager before the renewal deadline.
