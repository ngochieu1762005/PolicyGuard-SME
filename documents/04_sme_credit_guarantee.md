Credit guarantee mechanisms can support SMEs that have feasible business plans but face difficulty meeting collateral or credit requirements. Public policy documents describe credit guarantee funds as mechanisms that can provide guarantees for eligible small and medium-sized enterprises.

For an SME, this topic is relevant when the business needs financing but lacks sufficient collateral. The bank may need to review the business plan, financial situation, repayment capacity, revenue proof, and credit-related documents.

Recommended actions include preparing a business plan, cash flow report, revenue proof, loan purpose statement, and repayment plan. The impact is especially high for SMEs applying for new credit or renewing a working capital loan.
