Decree 123/2020/ND-CP regulates the management and use of invoices and records for goods sale, service provision, and tax-related procedures. Businesses are expected to use legal invoices and manage invoice creation and issuance according to the required rules.

For SMEs, invoice compliance is important because invoice records are often used as evidence of revenue, sales activity, and tax compliance. Missing or inconsistent invoice records can affect tax preparation and may also delay loan review or loan renewal when the bank asks for revenue proof.

Recommended actions for affected SMEs include checking e-invoice registration status, reviewing sales invoice records, keeping digital invoice files organized, and preparing invoice summaries before loan renewal or tax submission.
