title: Tax Payment and Penalty Risk
topic: tax
source_id: SRC003
---

Tax compliance risk can create real financial costs for businesses. Public tax guidance for Vietnam commonly highlights that underdeclared tax may lead to penalties, and late payment may create daily interest charges. Serious tax evasion can lead to much heavier penalties.

For SMEs, tax risk is not only a legal issue. It can also affect banking relationships because banks may request tax payment proof, tax declarations, or accounting records during credit review. If tax documents are missing or inconsistent, the loan process may take longer.

Recommended actions include preparing tax declarations, keeping tax payment proof, reviewing revenue records, and resolving late payment issues before applying for a new loan or renewing an existing loan.
