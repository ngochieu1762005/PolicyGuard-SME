# Public Data Sources

The demo dataset was prepared from public information and rewritten into short policy notes for RAG demonstration.

1. Law on Support for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises No. 04/2017/QH14
2. Decree 123/2020/ND-CP on invoices and records
3. Vietnam Tax Booklet 2025 by Andersen Vietnam
4. OECD SME and Entrepreneurship Policy in Viet Nam
5. Decision 58/2013/QD-TTg on Credit Guarantee Funds for SMEs

The app uses these sources as public policy context only. All SME profiles are synthetic demo data.
